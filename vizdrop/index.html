Questions, answered

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. VizDrop runs entirely in your browser — your file is read on your own device and never sent to a server. Close the tab and it’s gone.

What files can I use?

Excel (.xlsx, .xls), CSV, TSV, and JSON. Multi-sheet workbooks are supported — you can switch between sheets. Messy files with title rows, currency symbols, and mixed date formats are handled automatically.

Do I need to know anything about charts?

No. Drop a file and VizDrop builds the dashboard for you. If you want to tweak, every chart has a simple edit menu — change the type, fields, or colors with two clicks.

How do I get Pro after paying?

You’ll receive a license key by email right after checkout. Paste it into the app once and Pro stays active on that browser.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — it’s a monthly subscription with no lock-in. Cancel in one click from your receipt email and you won’t be billed again.
